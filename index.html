01 // what we do

We build networks of AI agents that work as a team, each with a clear job, connected to each other and to the systems you already run.

> design

Map the work. Decide which agents you need, what each one owns, and where a human stays in the loop.

> integrate

Connect agents to your stack: chat, email, docs, calendars, CMS, CRM, and internal APIs. No rip-and-replace.

> operate

Run, monitor, and tune the network after launch so it keeps doing the job as your business changes.

// built for media, content, and operations teams with more recurring work than people to do it.

02 // how an engagement works

  1. [1]

    discover

    A working session on your workflows, tools, and pain points. We pick the highest-value place to start.

  2. [2]

    pilot

    A small agent team on one real workflow, connected to your actual tools, with clear success criteria.

  3. [3]

    integrate

    Harden what works, add guardrails and handoffs, and expand to the next workflows.

  4. [4]

    run

    Ongoing operation and improvement, or a clean handoff to your team. Your call.
